Four parallel digital receivers search each RF window. Stable video sync triggers grayscale frame capture.

One range per line, up to 8. Example: 5650-5950. Use 5800-5800 to stay on one frequency.

Fine grid: more observation time, smaller carrier offsets.

RF LO + four digital tuners

61.44 MSPS input, 15.36 MSPS per DDC. Search centers span up to 40 MHz per LO. The LO stays fixed while digital tuning fills the grid. RF settling applies only when RF settings change.

A coarse grid is faster but may miss off-center signals. Try a 2 MHz grid and longer observation around a suspected frequency. 40 MHz is a center scheduling limit, not guaranteed detection bandwidth.

PAL / NTSC is confirmed using FPGA video sync and a validated DMA frame. 70–6000 MHz is the software tuning range; usable frequencies depend on the E200 RF front end and antenna.
